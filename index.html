SYSTEM NOTES ▸ (tap to expand)
  • Magic Chain: Light → Heavy → Special can cancel recovery frames on hit/block.
  • Block: Hold Guard — reduced chip damage and brief blockstun. Throws beat block!
  • Corner Break: After 4 hits or blocks near a solid wall, HOLD Guard (P1 C, P2 M, controller Guard, or touch GUARD) to break stun and push the attacker away. Free even with no chakra. Brief protection lets you move, jump or roll out; attacking ends it. Wall-splat extends a combo only once.
  • Throw: Light+Heavy together up close — hold toward them for a forward toss or hold away for an over-shoulder back throw. Beats block. Ember's forward throw is a claw SLICE — they fly forward and tumble along the ground.
  • Ember Up Attack: Hold Up + Heavy on the ground — rising double-claw launcher. Sends them HIGH for a juggle.
  • Weapon clash: Two active weapon swings meeting at once cancel with sparks, recoil and a metal ring.
  • Mist Ambush: Mizu's mist hides her completely. A Heavy thrown from inside her own cloud is unblockable and hits harder — but it SPENDS the mist, hit or whiff — the cloud is gone and she fades back into view over about two thirds of a second. Guard won't save you; a Corner Break still will.
  • Kubikiri (the dark room): The Broken Hall's left wall is a partition. A clean hit hard enough — a throw does it in one — smashes a body through into the Sutra Store, and only that fighter goes in. Inside the dark they vanish; the chaser gets nothing but dust and floorboard creaks, and only while they move. Step in blind and a Heavy from the dark is unblockable, comes out on one frame and hits harder than the mist. So check first: a low sweep or a thrown blade into the dark switches it off, and any hit that finds the hider drags them into the light. Mizu is blind — the dark hides nothing from her.
  • Blade lock: Two steel edges meeting on the ground don't cancel — they LOCK. Mash Light/Heavy: lead by SIX presses and you break their guard and stand over a free hit — but never in the first 0.3s, however fast you mash. Neither leads, both recoil.
  • Shunshin: Double-tap a direction to dash (15 chakra). Let go after the opening burst to slide out of it (any button cuts the slide); reverse to turn; hold to travel farther. You can be clipped mid-dash.
  • Air control: Hold a direction when a launch connects to influence its angle. Heavier fighters travel less. Neutral air Light cancels on landing.
  • Executioner: Forward+Light — Shadow Slice. Forward+Special — Smoke-Bomb Shadow Strike. On a clean hit, cancel into jump, dash, or Special during their brief windows.
  • Winded: Hit 0 chakra and you pant for 1.5s — half speed, no jutsu. Budget your bar!
  • Kicks (0 chakra, work winded): Down+Light sweep (trips low) · Fwd+Light push kick (shoves, wall-splats, PUNTS decoys) · Back+Light heel turn (hits behind you).
  • 2nd Specials: Executioner Down+S HARAI OTOSHI (long low sweeping cut, trips) · Guard + Heavy SHADOW SLIP REVERSAL (i-frame slip back; it only counters if he read your swing) · Heavy right after a BLOCK = IRON GUARD REPRISAL (riposte; at DREAD 3 it comes out as the Execution; Tsubasa has his own, below) · Mizu Down+S in her mist = fake Mizus · Shin Fwd+S wire shuriken (yanks you in) · Tsubasa Fwd+S pass-through slash · Heavy right after a BLOCK = CRIMSON FANG (reversal counter — the guard is the wind-up, the answer is the red cross) · Ember S on wall pounce dive · Kael Down+S rising launcher (invulnerable start).
  • Signature Techniques: Executioner Back+S Iai Quick-Draw (one flash from the sheath — huge, punishable on whiff; Back+H also works) · Ember Back+S Blade-Trap Parry (crossed claws catch a swing, X-shred answers) · Mizu Up+S Vaulting Staff Slam (pole-vault forward, the bo detonates the floor) · Shin Down+S Shuriken Fan (three stars, one sweep) · Kael Back+S Niten Parry (wakizashi catches, katana answers) · Tsubasa Back+S Tanto Flurry (four rapid rakes, the last pops you up).
  • Crouch: Hold Down on the ground — duck under shuriken and high arcs (smaller target).
  • Dodge Roll: Hold Block + tap a direction (or hold Down + double-tap one) — FREE low roll that crosses them and ducks shuriken mid-move. Intangible across the middle only: time it and you pass through the hit, start it late and you get caught. Committed: no attacking out of it, and the recovery is punishable. Rolling again straight away staggers the window shorter. Air jump = auto backflip.
  • Mouse Mode: Toggle 🖱️ MOUSE P1 — your ninja follows the cursor (high cursor = jump), Click light, R-Click heavy, both = throw, Wheel special. Poof stays on C.
  • Hand Mode (experimental): Toggle 📷 HAND P1 — LEFT hand moves (slide/flick/raise/drop, fist = guard), RIGHT hand attacks (✊ light, ✌ heavy, ☝ special, 🤏 poof, both fists = throw). Needs webcam + internet; expect some lag.
  • Walls: Cling & wall-jump; clinging drains chakra after 1s. Sword Pogo (S + Atk in air) refreshes jumps — and the bounce now scales with your fall speed: dive faster, rebound higher.
  • Meteor Break: S + Heavy in air — a hang beat, then a gravity-anchored plunge. Spikes airborne foes into a ground-bounce re-pop; landing detonates a shockwave BOTH sides. Whiff it and the landing lag gets you punished.
  • Zero-G Cut: a neutral air Light hangs you in zero gravity for the swing (2 per airtime) — float over anti-airs, stitch juggle strings, then Meteor Break to finish.
  • Air Up-Poke: W + Light in air — every fighter thrusts their weapon straight UP (Ember rakes claws). Pops the target for a juggle; pairs with Meteor Break coming down.
  • Rounds: Best of 3, 90s each — HP leader takes a time-over round.
