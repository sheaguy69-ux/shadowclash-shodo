## BLOCK A — the character and the build (always paste this first)

> Chibi warrior in a clean 2D cel-illustration style — heavy black outlines, flat
> controlled shading, no pixel art, no 3D, no painterly rendering. Roughly 3 heads
> tall. **Strict side profile, facing LEFT.** Plain white background.
>
> **THE BUILD — this is the most important instruction.** He is the founder: the
> single heaviest, most imposing body in the roster. **His shoulders are the widest
> part of him by a wide margin — they span about two and a half of his own head
> widths**, so his head sits down BETWEEN his shoulders rather than perched on top
> of them. The silhouette is an inverted triangle: a deep slab of a chest and a
> broad shoulder line tapering to a comparatively narrow waist, then heavy planted
> legs. His neck is thick and short, swallowed by the trapezius. His upper arms are
> as thick as his thighs, and the cloth wraps on his forearms add bulk rather than
> clinging. His hands are large. The tattered mantle hangs from that wide shoulder
> line with real weight, falling in heavy folds that add volume at the flanks — it
> is a heavy cloak, not a thin cape stuck to his back.
>
> **Do NOT make him taller, and do NOT enlarge his head — his height and head are
> already correct.** All of the added mass goes into shoulders, chest, arms and
> thighs. If he could be mistaken for a slim, agile ninja scaled up, the drawing is
> wrong. He should look like he cannot fit through a doorway square-on.
>
> **IDENTITY (exact, no deviation):** a **WHITE SKULL MASK** with **three
> claw-scratch gouges** raked across it and **glowing RED eyes**. **Two dark horns.**
> A **BLACK HOOD** over a **tattered black mantle**. **Ash-gray worn plate armour**
> at the chest, bracers, knees and boots. **Cloth wrappings on BOTH hands and
> forearms.** **His RIGHT hand wears a CLAW — five long dark-gunmetal blades. His
> LEFT hand is wrapped and EMPTY, with no claw.** **Two swords are carried crossed
> on his back.** Palette: black, ash gray, white mask, red eyes — nothing else.
>
> **NEVER:** a mane or long flowing hair, a bone pelt, any purple, claws on both
> hands, extra arms, a ground shadow, a second character in frame, captions, frame
> numbers or panel borders.

## BLOCK B — the IDLE (2 drawings) · do this one first

Highest value in the whole list: the idle is on screen most of the match, so his
build is judged almost entirely on it.

> Draw TWO drawings of him standing at the ready, side by side on one page with a
> clear white gap between them, each figure 350–450px tall.
>
> 1. **IDLE, settled** — a wide planted stance, feet well apart, weight even, the
>    clawed right hand loose and slightly forward, the wrapped left hand closed near
>    the hip. Shoulders squared and enormous, chest plate proud, chin low so the
>    mask reads out of the shadow of the hood.
> 2. **IDLE, breathing in** — the same stance a moment later: chest lifted, the
>    shoulder line risen slightly, the mantle settling. Same size, same footing —
>    only the ribcage and mantle move.
>
> Both drawings must show the same body at the same scale.

## BLOCK C — the five claw-fix drawings

These currently read as if he has talons on both hands, which is wrong — the claw
is his right hand only.

> Draw these five poses, one page, clear white gaps, 350–450px tall each. In every
> one, the **right hand's five-bladed claw must be unmistakable, and the left hand
> must be plainly WRAPPED AND EMPTY** — no blades, no talon shapes, no ambiguity.
>
> 1. **Crouched ready stance** — low, coiled, claw forward.
> 2. **Rising off the floor** — pushing up off one knee after being knocked down.
> 3. **Axe kick** — one leg raised high and driving down heel-first.
> 4. **Claw thrust, first beat** — the clawed right arm cocked back to strike.
> 5. **Rising slash, second beat** — the claw carving upward past his own shoulder.

## BLOCK D — how to send it back

One move per page. White background. Figures 350–450px tall (bigger is fine —
everything gets scaled DOWN, never up). No captions under the figures, no numerals,
no panel rules, no dummy or opponent, no ground shadow.

**When it comes back I measure the shoulder span in head-widths before anything is
packed.** Target ≈ 2.4. He currently sits at 1.69, the Executioner at 2.48.
