a **shadow self** (if your Wholeness is chipped) → the three doors (**Exile → Mokurai → Oni**)
→ your ending.

1. [B] The light did not go out. It was taken — pulled down into a place under the world, and hung there like a lantern over a pit.
2. [B] Whoever holds it holds everything: name, memory, the world above.
3. [B] So the pit calls six who already have blood between them — and gives them one rule.
4. [B] Only one walks out with the light. The rest stay dark.
5. [B] But the light is not guarded by rules. It is guarded by three. They cannot win. They are the doors.

## SHADOW SELF SCENES — the curse's first touch

> **Mechanic (brief):** the shadow is your exact double — runtime clone, same kit, dark shader,
> dialogue table. No new moveset, no bespoke AI. It escalates — needles at tier 1, accuses at tier 2,
> states it flatly at tier 3 — and every return is faster and stronger. It is the only honest voice in
> the room, and the only one you can't dismiss, because it's you.

- [V] KAEL's shadow: "You never asked him why. You still haven't."
- [V] TSUBASA's shadow: "You're not protecting Kael. You're protecting yourself from having to see his face when he finds out."
- [V] THE EXECUTIONER's shadow: "You didn't obey an order. You wanted permission."
- [V] MIZU's shadow: "You didn't calculate. You froze — and you've been calling it arithmetic ever since."
- [V] SHIN's shadow: "It was never about the money. They never respected you, and you wanted them to lose something."
- [V] EMBER's shadow: "You didn't come back to prove them wrong. You came back so they'd have to look at you."

> It also mocks the realm: *"this place eats people like you; it's already started"* — which is true,
> and the only warning the six get.

### Three hard rules (binding)
1. **Never reveals plot** — only interior truth the character already knows.
2. **May circle the death-doubt, never confirm it** — *"you don't sleep, do you."*
3. **Stays opposite the Sage** — the Sage offers relief and is wrong about you; the shadow offers
   nothing and is exactly right. Warm and blind vs. cold and accurate.
