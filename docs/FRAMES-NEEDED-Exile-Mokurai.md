# FRAMES NEEDED — EXILE + MOKURAI

Every frame missing from either fighter, described pose by pose.

**84 frames total — Exile 43, Mokurai 41.**

All frames: chibi proportions (~3 heads tall), 2D cel illustration — heavy black outlines,
flat controlled palette, readable silhouette. **Not pixel art.** Strict side profile unless

# CHARACTER 1 — EXILE

**Who she is:** the fastest body on the roster and its highest jumper. A glass cannon —
every pose reads reach and speed, never weight.

**Build:** chibi ninja, ~3 heads tall, slight and light.

**Head:** no hood. A huge windswept **black mane with silver-white streaks**, long, always
streaming behind her — it is the loudest shape in her silhouette. A **tan cloth eye-wrap
over one eye with dark-red kanji brushed on it**. A **black cloth mask** over nose and
mouth. One visible eye: pale, hard, under a heavy dark brow.

**Body:** black and charcoal ninja garb — wrapped sleeves, layered skirt panels over black
trousers. A **deep purple scarf** at the neck and **two long purple sash tails** off the
waist that trail and whip. **Tan bandage wraps** on forearms, hands, shins and ankles — a
real secondary colour against all the black.

**Weapons — she carries both at all times:**
- **Kama sickle** in her lead hand: silver curved blade, dark wrapped handle, red-brown grip.
- **Chain with a round smooth iron weight ball** in her other hand. Links must be readable
  individually along the whole length.

**Chain rules — the thing most likely to go wrong:**
- **Thrown or extended → the chain is a STRAIGHT TAUT LINE.** Ball at full stretch. No sag.
- **Hanging or circling → the chain SAGS** in a natural hanging curve. A stiff straight
  chain at rest looks like a metal rod and is wrong.
- **The chain is one continuous unbroken object** from her fist to the ball in every single
  frame. It never breaks, fades out mid-length, or jumps position between frames.
- The ball is a **round smooth iron weight** — never a blade, never spiked.
- The sickle stays in the same hand always. Never two sickles, never two balls.

**Never:** a hood · a faceless void where her face is · both eyes uncovered · a katana or
kunai · red eyes · gold or red effects · a heavy planted stance.

## EXILE — ANIMATIONS

### 1. LOW SICKLE SLASH — 4 frames
*A crouching armed cut along the floor. Currently she draws a plain crouch here, no attack.*

1. **Drop.** Weight slams down onto a deep bent front knee, back leg trailing straight out
   behind her, shin flat to the ground. Sickle hand pulled back low by her hip, blade
   turned so the curve faces forward. Ball tucked in against her body. Mane thrown forward
   over her shoulder by the sudden drop. Sash tails still high, catching up.
2. **Sweep.** The sickle arm fires forward at ankle height, blade leading, arm not yet
   straight. Torso twists into it, shoulder dropping toward the floor. Free hand plants
   flat on the ground for balance, chain pooling in a loose coil beside that hand. Mane
   sweeping down and forward.
3. **Full extension.** Sickle arm completely straight, blade tip at the far reach of the
   sweep, still at ankle height — the money frame. Body stretched long and low, back leg
   fully extended. Chain snapped taut behind her from the planted hand. Mane and both sash
   tails streaming straight back, the whole silhouette one long horizontal line.
4. **Recover.** She hauls back onto her heels, sickle arm folding in across her chest,
   knees rising toward a crouch. Chain slackens into a sag. Mane collapsing back down over
   her shoulders. Weight settling, not yet standing.

### 2. AIRBORNE CHAIN HURL — 4 frames
*Mid-air, she throws the ball out sideways. Currently one single frame covers the whole move.*

1. **Wind.** Body turned slightly away, knees tucked up. The ball is drawn back behind her
   shoulder, chain gathered in a loose sagging loop. Sickle arm folded across her chest.
   Mane blown upward and back by the fall.
2. **Release.** Torso whips open, throwing shoulder driving forward. The ball leaves her
   fist, chain beginning to straighten but still holding one shallow curve near her hand.
   Legs split apart for counterbalance — one tucked, one reaching down.
3. **Taut.** Chain a dead-straight line from her fist out to the ball at full reach, every
   link crisp. Ball at the far end, spikes clear. Her body counter-leans hard away from the
   throw, spine arched. Sash tails and mane pulled toward her, opposite the throw.
4. **Slack.** The chain goes loose and sags into a deep curve, ball dropping. She folds
   back toward centre, knees drawing up, sickle arm coming back to guard. Mane settling.

### 3. UP REACH — 5 frames
*Planted on the ground, chain hurled straight up as an anti-air. She never leaves the floor.*

1. **Plant.** Both feet flat and wide, knees bent, weight dropped straight down into the
   ground. Chin lifting, the visible eye angled upward. Ball held low in her fist, chain
   pooled at her feet in loose coils. Mane settling downward.
2. **Whip.** The throwing arm rips upward past her ear. The ball leaves the ground, chain
   lifting out of its coil in an S-curve, links flying. Her body stretches tall, up onto
   the balls of her feet. Sickle arm swings out wide for counterbalance.
3. **Vertical and taut.** The chain is a straight vertical line from her raised fist to the
   ball high above her head — a tall narrow silhouette. Feet still planted flat, knees
   locked, spine stretched. Mane pulled straight up alongside the chain. **The reach frame.**
4. **Fall.** The ball drops back down past her. The chain buckles into a lazy S as tension
   leaves it. Her raised arm bends, elbow dropping. Weight sinking back into her knees.
5. **Catch.** She snatches the ball back into her fist at chest height, chain sagging in a
   deep loop below. Knees soft, head levelling out, mane falling back over her shoulders.

### 4. UP-FLICK — 3 frames
*A short fast poke straight up. Distinct from Up Reach: no chain, sickle only, over in a blink.*

1. **Chamber.** A tight compact crouch, sickle drawn back beside her ear, blade turned
   upward. Ball held tucked at her hip, chain short and slack. Barely any wind-up — this is
   a fast move and the pose should look coiled, not committed.
2. **Flick.** Sickle arm snaps straight up above her head, blade tip at the top of the
   frame, curve pointing back over her. Body extends onto her toes. Mane kicked upward.
   Chain and ball stay tucked and quiet — they take no part in this.
3. **Snap back.** Arm folds straight back down to guard, sickle across her chest. Heels
   drop. Mane falls. She is already back in a neutral ready stance.

### 5. COUNTER-WEIGHT SNAP — 5 frames
*A back-step throw that covers a ring at mid-range and nothing up close. She retreats into it.*

1. **Shift.** Weight rocks back onto the rear foot, front foot going light on its toe. The
   ball swings back behind her hip on a sagging chain. Sickle raised across her body,
   guarding. Mane starts to swing forward as her body goes back.
2. **Lean.** Torso leans hard away, spine curving back, chin tucked. The ball swings out
   wide and low behind her, chain still curved. Front leg extends forward off the ground,
   toe pointed. Sash tails whipping forward.
3. **Snap.** The ball fires out and forward past her extended front foot, chain straightening
   fast, one last shallow kink near her hand. Her body is at its furthest lean-back, almost
   diagonal, one hand low behind for balance.
4. **Extension held.** Chain fully taut and level, ball out at mid-range. Her body a hard
   backward-leaning diagonal — as far from the ball as she can get. Mane and sash streaming
   forward toward the ball, opposing her lean. **The money frame.**
5. **Haul back.** She yanks the chain in and rights herself; the chain crosses her body in
   a slack diagonal, ball swinging toward her hip. Front foot lands. Mane collapsing back.

### 6. AIR DOWN-POKE — 2 frames
*A short downward stab in the air. Not the big dive-smash — a small quick jab.*

1. **Tuck.** Knees pulled up tight to her chest, body compact and ball-like. Sickle turned
   point-down beneath her. Ball held close at her side, chain short and slack. Mane pushed
   straight up by the fall.
2. **Stab.** Sickle arm drives straight down below her, blade tip at the lowest point.
   Legs kick out and back behind her, body angled forward over the strike. Chain flicks out
   behind. Mane streaming straight up.

### 7. LIGHT CHAIN — BEATS 3, 4, 5 — 3 frames
*The back half of her five-hit combo. All three currently draw the same single picture.*

3. **Cross-cut.** Sickle slashes across her body low-to-high on a diagonal, arm at three-
   quarter extension, blade in front of her chest. Front foot stamped forward, weight over
   it. Torso rotated into the cut, opposite shoulder pulled back. Mane whipping across
   behind the blade.
4. **Rising cut.** The sickle carries on upward past her shoulder, blade above head height,
   curve tipped back. She rises onto her toes, spine stretching, chest open. The ball
   swings out and up on a curved chain, following the rotation. Mane pulled upward.
5. **Spin finisher.** Full body rotation — she has turned through, hips leading. The sickle
   sweeps around at chest height, arm fully extended. The ball travels wide on a taut chain
   on the opposite side, so the two weapons balance the frame left and right. Sash tails and
   mane spiralling. The widest, most committed pose of the chain.

### 8. IDLE BREATH — 3 frames
*Her idle is currently one static frame. These three plus the existing one make a 4-frame loop.*

1. **Settle.** Slightly lower than neutral — knees a touch more bent, shoulders down.
   Sickle low at her side. Ball hanging still, chain in a deep quiet sag. Mane at rest,
   only the tips lifting.
2. **Inhale.** Chest rises, shoulders lift, spine lengthens by a hair. The ball drifts
   outward, chain swinging into a shallower curve. Mane lifting, sash tails floating out.
3. **Exhale.** Chest falls, shoulders drop. The ball drifts back the other way, chain
   swinging past its resting point to a slightly deeper curve on the near side. Mane
   settling back down.

*Motion is tiny — the chain and mane carry the life. The body barely moves.*

### 9. RUN — PHASES 5-8 — 4 frames
*She has four run frames doing the work of eight. These are the missing four, mirroring the
existing set onto the opposite leg. Torso stays LOW and near-horizontal — she is a sprinter.*

5. **Contact, opposite leg.** The other foot strikes the ground ahead, leg straight, heel
   first. Rear leg extended fully behind, toe trailing. Torso pitched far forward, almost
   horizontal. Sickle arm swept back, blade flat. Ball dragging low behind, chain nearly
   straight. Mane a long horizontal banner.
6. **Down, opposite leg.** Front knee bends deep, absorbing weight, hips at their lowest.
   Rear leg starts to fold up. Torso lowest here. Arms at their widest spread.
7. **Pass, opposite leg.** The rear leg swings through beside the standing leg, knee high
   and folded, feet crossing — a true pass pose, both legs readable and not merged. Hips
   rising. Arms swap: sickle arm swinging forward, ball arm back.
8. **Up, opposite leg.** Standing leg pushes to full extension, up on the toe, hips at
   their highest. Front knee driving forward and up. Torso still low and forward. Chain
   flicking up behind. The frame with the most lift, straight into frame 1.

### 10. DODGE ROLL — 4 frames
*Currently draws her jump apex pose. Needs a real tumble.*

1. **Duck.** She drops and commits forward, shoulder leading, chin tucked to her chest,
   arms folding in. Sickle turned flat against her forearm so it does not lead. Ball pulled
   in tight, chain wrapped short. Mane thrown forward over her head.
2. **Tuck.** Fully balled up, back curved, knees to chest, leading shoulder reaching the
   ground. Nearly circular silhouette. Chain wrapped around the tucked shape. Mane fanned
   out around her.
3. **Inverted.** Upside down at the top of the roll, feet above her head, back rounded,
   still compact. Sickle and ball held tight to her body — neither weapon flails. Mane a
   ring around the tucked form.
4. **Rise out.** Feet come down and plant, still low in a deep crouch, body uncurling, one
   hand touching the floor. Sickle swinging back out to guard. Chain unwrapping into a
   sag. Mane whipping back over her shoulders as she comes up.

### 11. JUMP ARC — 6 frames
*She is the highest jumper in the game and currently has a 4-frame jump with no rotation.
This is a full acrobatic arc.*

1. **Crouch launch.** Deep compression — knees fully bent, hips low, both feet flat,
   arms drawn down and back. The most compact standing pose she has. Chain pooled at her
   feet. Mane pressed down.
2. **Rise.** Both legs snap straight and together, body stretched into a long vertical
   line, arms coming up. Toes pointed. Chain lifting off the ground in a trailing curve.
   Mane and sash pulled straight down by the speed.
3. **Tuck.** Knees pull up to her chest, body compacting into a ball, beginning to rotate
   backward. Sickle and ball drawn in tight. Mane starting to wrap around the rotation.
4. **Apex inversion.** Fully upside down at the top of the arc, tightest tuck, feet over
   head. Both weapons held close. Mane fanned in a full circle around her. **She is at her
   smallest and most compact here — do not stretch this frame.**
5. **Open out.** The tuck releases, legs unfold and reach down toward the ground, arms
   spreading wide. Body stretched long again, rotating upright. Chain swinging out below
   her. Mane streaming upward.
6. **Landing.** Both feet contact together, knees deep, absorbing — a compressed crouch
   with the torso upright and arms out for balance. Chain slapping down beside her. Mane
   collapsing forward over her shoulders.

**Total: 43 frames.**

# CHARACTER 2 — MOKURAI

**Who he is:** a silent monk who fights bare-handed and hands your own force back. Heavy,
planted, unhurried — the opposite of Exile. He does not scramble; he arrives.

> **One thing to settle first.** His existing sheet contains two different head designs: a
> **grey cracked-stone** head on seven older cells, and a **green-jade face under a stone
> skullcap** on everything else, which is the version carrying almost his whole moveset.
> The descriptions below use the **green-jade** version, since that is what most of the
> shipped art already is. If you want the grey stone head instead, say so — the seven old
> cells would then be the ones to redo, and the description below changes only at the head.

**Build:** chibi monk, ~3 heads tall, solid and grounded — broad through the chest and hips.

**Head:** bald. A **green-jade face** under a **grey cracked-stone skullcap**, a **gold band
across the temple with a round ruby set into it**, a gold ear-guard, and a **glowing gold
eye**, angled and hard.

**Body:** a **saffron / ochre-gold robe** with gold trim and a dark geometric key pattern at
the hem, worn over **dark brown baggy trousers**. A **deep red scarf** at the neck that
streams back — his loudest moving shape. A **red sash** knotted at the waist. **Dark wooden
prayer-bead strands** wrapped around both wrists and hanging at his chest. Pale green-white
hands, bare feet in low green-grey sandals.

**Weapons: none. He is bare-handed.** Bead-wrapped fists and open palms only.

**Never:** a staff of any kind · any weapon at all · a demon mask · the grey stone head
mixed into a green sequence · circular / orbiting / spinning-halo posing — it makes his
prayer beads read as a swinging flail, which is wrong. His strikes are **straight lines**:
straight down, straight forward, straight up.

## MOKURAI — ANIMATIONS

### 1. AIRBORNE STRIKE — 5 frames
*His single worst gap: in the air his heavy currently draws his block pose, his jump pose
and his idle. He has no airborne attack art at all.*

1. **Gather.** Mid-air, knees drawn up, both fists pulled in tight to his chest, elbows
   down. Body compact and coiled. Robe billowing upward around him. Scarf whipping over one
   shoulder. Chin down, gold eye fixed below him.
2. **Drive.** Both arms fire down and forward together, elbows straightening. Body opens
   from the ball shape, chest turning toward the target. Legs kick back behind him for
   counterweight. Robe hem snapping up. Beads swinging out along the arms.
3. **Contact.** Both arms fully extended down-forward, fists at the far point, knuckles
   leading. Body stretched into a long committed diagonal, one straight line from the
   trailing foot through the shoulders to the fists. Scarf pulled straight back behind him.
   **The money frame.**
4. **Follow-through.** Arms carry past the contact point and begin to drop below him. His
   torso rotates slightly forward over the strike, hips coming up behind. Robe folding
   around the rotation. Beads trailing.
5. **Recover.** Arms fold back in toward his chest, legs swing down and reach for the
   ground, toes pointed down. Body returning upright in the air. Robe settling.

### 2. THROW — 3 frames
*Currently drawn with his block pose followed by his jump pose. Needs a real grab.*

1. **Seize.** Both arms punch forward, hands open and grasping at chest height. He steps in
   onto a bent front knee, weight over it. Shoulders square and driving. Beads swing forward
   past his wrists. Scarf flaring behind.
2. **Haul.** Hips rotate hard, both arms sweeping across his body to one side, dragging
   weight with them. He drops low over a deeply bent knee, back braced, spine coiled. Robe
   twisting hard around the hips, scarf whipping across his chest.
3. **Release.** Full rotation through — hips and shoulders squared to the far side, arms
   thrown open and out, hands releasing. Chest up and open, head following the throw. Robe
   and scarf flung out wide. Feet planted, having pivoted.

### 3. STEPPING PALM — 5 frames
*A forward-advancing strike of its own. Currently just replays his neutral heavy.*

1. **Step in.** Front foot lands forward, weight transferring onto it, back leg extended
   behind. Lead shoulder rolls forward. Rear hand chambered at his hip, palm up. Lead arm
   loose in front. Scarf trailing behind the step.
2. **Load.** Weight fully over the front leg, hips beginning to rotate, rear elbow drawn
   back and up behind him — the arm coiled. Chest closed. Beads swinging back along the
   forearm.
3. **Drive.** The elbow leads forward, forearm following, hand still slightly open —
   halfway through, arm not yet straight. Hips squared. Rear heel lifting off the ground.
   Robe hem thrown back.
4. **Contact.** Arm fully extended forward, **palm flat and vertical, fingers up**, at
   chest height. Body stretched in a long line from the trailing rear toe through to the
   palm. Rear arm pulled back hard to his hip in counterbalance. Scarf snapped straight
   back. Beads flung forward along the striking arm. **The money frame.**
5. **Settle.** Arm bends and draws back toward centre, weight rocking back between his
   feet. Shoulders dropping. Robe and scarf falling. He is returning to a rooted stance,
   not a light one.

### 4. LOW HAMMER — 5 frames
*A downward strike to the floor. Currently replays his neutral heavy.*

1. **Sink.** Both knees bend and hips drop straight down, feet wide and flat. Both fists
   rise together above one shoulder, stacked. Chin tucks, eye on the floor. Robe compressing.
2. **Peak.** Fists at their highest, arms nearly straight overhead, body stretched tall
   through the ribs even as the knees stay bent. Beads hanging straight down from the raised
   wrists. Scarf lifted.
3. **Drop.** Both fists drive straight down, arms passing his chest, elbows out. Hips sink
   further, knees folding deeper. Robe hem thrown upward by the speed of the descent.
4. **Impact.** Both fists strike the ground between his feet, arms fully extended downward,
   back rounded over them, hips at their lowest. A compressed, heavy, wide silhouette — all
   the weight in the world going into the floor. Scarf snapped upward. Beads bouncing off
   the wrists. **The money frame.**
5. **Rise.** He pushes back up off the floor, fists lifting to chest height, spine
   straightening, knees extending toward a rooted stance. Robe settling down around him.

### 5. DIVING FIST — 3 frames
*The air-down attack. Currently draws his ordinary falling pose.*

1. **Tuck.** At the top: knees pull up, both fists gather at his chest, body compacting.
   Chin down, looking straight below. Robe puffed out around the ball shape.
2. **Plunge.** Both arms drive straight down beneath him, fists together, legs extended
   straight up above — the whole body a **vertical line, head down**. Robe and scarf pulled
   hard upward past him. The most stretched pose in his kit.
3. **Landing compression.** Feet slam down, knees fully folded, hips near the ground, fists
   still low in front of him. Squashed wide and low. Robe flared out at the hem, scarf
   thrown forward over his shoulder.

### 6. BELL RINGER — 5 frames
*A planted anti-air: he drives a palm at the sky and does not leave the ground. Currently one
single frame, shared with the palm blast.*

1. **Root.** Feet plant wide and flat, knees bend, weight drops. Both hands drawn in low at
   his waist, palms up. Head tilts back, gold eye up. Robe settling heavily.
2. **Chamber.** The striking hand pulls back to his hip, palm turned up and fingers spread.
   Opposite arm crosses his chest as a guard. Shoulders coil away from the strike. Beads
   gathered at the chambered wrist.
3. **Drive.** The arm fires upward past his ear, elbow leading, hand opening. Body
   stretching tall, spine extending, heels beginning to lift. Robe hem lifting.
4. **Full extension.** Arm locked straight overhead, **palm flat and open, fingers up**, at
   the very top of the frame. Body one long vertical column, feet still flat on the ground —
   he has stretched, not jumped. Scarf pulled straight up alongside the arm. Beads slid down
   toward the elbow. **The money frame.**
5. **Fall and settle.** Arm drops back down across his chest, shoulders sink, knees rebend
   into his rooted stance. Robe and scarf falling back around him.

### 7. PALM BLAST — 5 frames
*The big slow two-handed palm thrust. Currently shares its one frame with the Bell Ringer.*

1. **Gather.** Both palms drawn in to face each other in front of his chest, elbows wide,
   fingers spread — holding something between them. Weight even, knees soft. Head level.
   Beads hanging still from both wrists.
2. **Rock back.** Weight shifts onto the rear foot, front foot going light. Both palms pull
   further back toward his chest, elbows drawing behind his ribs. Chest closing, shoulders
   rolling forward. Scarf drifting forward as his body goes back.
3. **Thrust.** Both arms drive forward together, palms turning outward, elbows straightening.
   Weight slams onto the front foot. Chest opens. Robe hem thrown back hard.
4. **Locked.** Both arms fully extended forward at chest height, **palms flat, vertical,
   fingers up, wrists touching or nearly so**. Body stretched in one straight line from the
   rear toe to the palms. Scarf snapped straight back. Beads flung forward along both
   forearms. **The money frame — hold this one longest.**
5. **Recover.** Arms bend and draw back in toward his chest, weight rocking back to centre,
   shoulders dropping. Robe and scarf settling. Back to a rooted stance.

### 8. AIR PUNCH FORWARD — 2 frames
*In the air, holding forward. He currently has no directional air attacks at all.*

1. **Chamber.** Airborne, knees tucked, striking fist drawn back beside his ribs, other arm
   forward as a guard. Torso turned slightly away. Robe billowing up.
2. **Punch.** Lead arm fully extended forward, fist at the far point, knuckles leading.
   Body angled forward over the punch, legs trailing behind. Rear fist snapped back to the
   ribs. Scarf pulled straight back. Beads thrown forward down the striking arm.

### 9. AIR ELBOW BACK — 2 frames
*In the air, holding back. A reverse strike behind him.*

1. **Coil.** Airborne, body turned away from the direction he faces, near arm folded tight
   across his chest, elbow tucked. Knees drawn up. Scarf whipping across his front.
2. **Elbow.** The folded arm drives backward, **elbow leading**, arm still bent — this is an
   elbow, not a punch. Torso rotated to look back over that shoulder. Legs kicking forward
   to counterbalance. Robe twisting. Beads swinging back with the elbow.

### 10. DODGE ROLL — 4 frames
*Currently draws his falling pose.*

1. **Duck.** He drops forward, leading shoulder down, chin tucked, both arms folding in
   against his chest. Robe compressing around him. Scarf thrown forward over his head.
2. **Tuck.** Fully balled up, back rounded, knees to chest, shoulder reaching the ground.
   Nearly circular. Robe wrapped tight around the tuck. Beads pinned against his body.
3. **Inverted.** Upside down at the top of the roll, feet above his head, still compact.
   Robe hem hanging toward the ground. Scarf coiled around the tucked shape.
4. **Rise out.** Feet plant, body uncurling into a deep low crouch, one palm touching the
   floor for balance. Arms swinging back out to a guard. Robe and scarf falling back down.

### 11. WALL CLING — 2 frames
*Currently draws his block pose. Needs a real wall pose.*

1. **Catch.** Body flat and vertical against the wall, both palms pressed to it at shoulder
   height, fingers spread. Knees bent, both feet flat to the wall beneath him. Head turned
   to look down over his shoulder. Robe pressed flat against his back. Scarf hanging
   straight down.
2. **Slide.** Same contact but sinking — palms dragging upward relative to his body as he
   slips down, arms straightening above him, knees extending. Robe and scarf dragged
   **upward** by the descent. Chin lifting as he goes down.

**Total: 41 frames.**
