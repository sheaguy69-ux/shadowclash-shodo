## THE FOUR RULES (each one has caused a real re-do)

1. **Draw the fighter FACING LEFT.** The engine mirrors by facing; art drawn
   facing right renders backwards in play. This has bitten three fighters.
2. **No baked ground shadow.** The engine draws its own — and these are
   airborne poses anyway.
3. **No second character in the frame.** The engine draws the opponent.
4. **Real white gutters between panels.** Overlapping FX arcs make a page
   unsplittable. One move per page, six panels in a row (or two rows of
   three), each panel captioned with its slot name (`hneu1` … `hneu6`).
   White background. Keep the body the SAME SIZE in all six panels.

## The six beats (same spine for all four pages)

The house attack spine: coil → launch → smear → KIME → follow-through →
settle. Per the animation standard: the smear panel carries **3-4 ghosted
weapon exposures tracing the arc** (never one blur blob); the KIME panel shows
the weapon at FULL extension exactly where the hit lands; squash/stretch lives
on the action beats, the KIME stays strong and clean; the silhouette must
still read at thumbnail size.
