# ShadowClash — Character Voice Brief

Owner-requested, Aug 2 2026. Per-character voice direction grounded in the Story
Bible; app recommendations at the bottom (verified against current sources — see
the APPS section). Fighting-game VO is ~90% **barks** — efforts, kiai, hurt sounds,
KO cries — and ~10% spoken lines (intros, victories, taunts). Every character below
gets: how the voice SOUNDS, how it DELIVERS, and a paste-ready **casting prompt**
for a voice-design tool.

**The bark manifest (generate this set per fighter, ~20-25 clips each):**
- 4× light-attack efforts (short, varied) · 3× heavy efforts (bigger) · 2× special kiai
- 3× light hurt · 2× heavy hurt · 1× KO death cry · 1× low-HP breathing loop
- 2× jump/land breath · 1× roll effort · 2× taunt lines · 1× intro line · 2× victory lines

**Pronoun canon (never infer):** Kael he · Tsubasa he · Shin he · Ember he ·
Mizu she · Executioner he · Mokurai he · Exile/Kunoichi she.

# THE SIX

## KAEL — the last student
- **Sounds like:** warm male baritone, clear and even — the only voice in
  the pit with no damage in it. Breath control of a lifelong martial artist.
  **Youngest of the six — early 20s.**
- **Delivery:** steady, present, unhurried; conviction without volume. He still
  believes the light can be shared — hope should be audible and slightly stubborn.
  Efforts are clean dojo kiai, textbook and honest.
- **Casting prompt:** "Warm, grounded male baritone, early 20s, trained martial
  artist. Calm and sincere with quiet conviction. Clean unforced projection,
  disciplined breathing. No rasp, no cynicism."

## TSUBASA — precision as a religion
- **Sounds like:** cold male tenor, 30s, clipped consonants, surgical enunciation.
  Slightly lighter and sharper than Kael — the contrast matters, they share a past.
- **Delivery:** minimal words, held contempt, never raises his voice — even his
  kiai are compressed and exact, effort spent like currency. The resentment leaks
  as ice, not heat. His victory lines should sound like corrections.
- **Casting prompt:** "Cold, precise male tenor, mid-30s. Clipped, controlled,
  quietly contemptuous. Speaks softly with perfect diction; never shouts. The calm
  of someone waiting for you to make the mistake."

## SHIN — the wire, the mail, the staying quiet
- **Sounds like:** low male voice, 40s, worn gravel, close-mic quiet — a man who
  has spent a life not being heard.
- **Delivery:** flat, economical, tired knowledge; guilt sits under everything he
  says to or about Ember. Barks are MUTED — sharp exhales and grunts, not shouts;
  a man doing violence quietly. His KO cry is choked short, not screamed.
- **Casting prompt:** "Quiet, low male voice, mid-40s, gravel-worn and flat. Speaks
  in short, reluctant phrases, close and confidential, emotionally guarded with
  buried regret. Never theatrical, never loud."

## EMBER — what crawled out of the fire (he/him — canon)
- **Sounds like:** young male voice, raw and smoke-damaged — a permanent
  scorched crack on the edge of every sound. **Mid-20s** — older than Kael, and the
  smoke damage, not the age, is what makes him sound the way he does.
- **Delivery:** volatile — snarls, half-laughs that die mid-breath, words fighting
  through the throat. Efforts are wild and torn, almost animal; hurt sounds are the
  scariest in the roster because they sound like the fire again. Any line touching
  Shin carries betrayal barely held: rage that keeps almost becoming grief.
- **Casting prompt:** "Young male voice, mid-20s, raw, hoarse, smoke-damaged
  rasp. Feral energy, unstable — switches between snarling aggression and cracked
  vulnerability mid-sentence. Sounds like he's still inside the worst night of his
  life."

## MIZU — keeper of the drowned shrine
- **Sounds like:** composed female alto, 30s, smooth and cool, ritual cadence — a
  voice trained for ceremony, repurposed for war.
- **Delivery:** formal, measured, the calm of someone holding a line and a secret.
  Sad steel underneath — she knows what the lantern costs and says nothing. Barks
  are controlled and clean, like temple commands; her KO is the composure finally
  breaking, and it should be quiet, not shrieked. Lines about Kael get one degree
  warmer — she likes him, which makes it worse.
- **Casting prompt:** "Composed female alto, 30s, cool and ceremonial. Formal
  measured speech with an undertone of sorrow held in check. Priestess turned
  soldier: serene surface, steel underneath."

## THE EXECUTIONER — the law's last word
- **Sounds like:** deep male bass, resonant, **the oldest of the six** (50s, weathered,
  never frail) — and ALWAYS behind the mask.
  **TWO VOICES — owner, Sep 15 2026. Keep BOTH. Same man, mask on and mask off.**
  **MASKED (default, every in-fight line and bark):** the deep, almost demonic Voice
  Design take — it already reads masked on its own, so there is no post low-pass and no
  reverb. The owner picked it because it matches him; it is never to be replaced.
  **UNMASKED:** that same deepness humanised — a weathered man in his fifties, real
  breath and grain, no demonic edge. Held for the moments the mask is off.
- **Delivery:** verdict cadence — slow, final, no anger, no pleasure. He isn't
  fighting you, he's carrying out a sentence. Efforts are heavy exhales, not kiai.
  Taunts read like rulings. The one hint of a man under the mask: a fractional
  pause before killing blows, as if reading the warrant one last time.
- **Casting prompt — MASKED:** "Immense male bass, deep and resonant, speaking slowly
  with judicial finality. Emotionless authority — every sentence a verdict. Muffled
  slightly, as if behind a heavy mask." (Ship it clean — no post filter. The design
  carries the mask.)
- **Casting prompt — UNMASKED:** "Immense male bass, deep and resonant, speaking slowly
  with judicial finality. Emotionless authority — every sentence a verdict. Keep the same
  extreme deepness, but human: a weathered man in his fifties under the mask, real breath
  and grain in the low end. Not demonic, not monstrous, not processed"

# THE THREE

## MOKURAI — the second door — ⚠ CANON: HE SPEAKS (never mute)
- **He speaks constantly, gently, sincerely.** 黙雷 ("silent thunder") is his
  NATURE, not his voice — the silence that follows is what his mercy leaves
  behind, a hollowed-out person who does not know anything was taken.
- **Sounds like:** sub-deep male resonance, warm and unhurried, used for full
  speech — koans that land wrong, eulogies for people who still think they are
  breathing, weeping apologies mid-fight, and the mercy offer that is the
  ASCENSION gate.
- **Delivery:** every line is offered, never forced — no anger, no triumph, only
  patience. He is trying to give you something, and it will hollow you out.
- **Casting prompt:** "Extremely deep male voice, warm, slow, sincerely gentle —
  a monk who speaks to comfort you and never notices he is taking something. Full
  words, never wordless. Koans, eulogies, whispered apologies."

## EXILE — the first door, the toll-taker
- **Sounds like:** older female voice, 50s, dry and rasped, unhurried — the oldest
  voice in the pit and the least impressed. One functioning eye, zero surprises left.
- **Delivery:** amused-tired, a collector's politeness with a blade in it. She has
  swept up after every loser this pit ever ate — every line should sound like she
  already knows how your fight ends. Barks are sharp, short, precise (chain work is
  economy of motion); her taunt is a low single laugh. Never breathless — she
  fights from range and never spends more than she must.
- **Casting prompt:** "Dry, raspy female voice, 50s, unhurried and darkly amused.
  Speaks with weary authority and a collector's politeness — every line sounds
  like a toll being named. Economical, knowing, faintly dangerous."

## BONUS — THE PIT (announcer, optional)
- The ROUND/FIGHT/KO banners currently ring synth tones. If the pit itself gets a
  voice: cavernous, genderless, slightly slowed, more geology than person — the
  tournament's hunger given a mouth. Two words at a time, maximum.

# APPS — verified Aug 2 2026 (web-checked, not from memory)

## PRIMARY: ElevenLabs (native account — Starter $6/mo, Creator $22/mo for headroom)
The one app where voice CREATION, spoken lines, and directed barks live under one
roof:
- **Voice Design v3** builds a voice from a text description (the casting prompts
  above paste straight in), Character mode, 3 candidates per prompt. Save the pick
  to My Voices — that voice ID **is** the fighter forever. Consistency solved at
  the platform level, no actor, no reference audio.
- **Eleven v3 audio tags** direct the barks: `[shouts] HYAAA!`, `[grunts] Hnn!`,
  `[gasps] Ugh—`, `[screams] AAAGH…`, `[laughs]`. Tags are performed, not spoken.
- **Sound Effects model** = same-vendor fallback for fully wordless clips.
- Commercial license starts at Starter ($6/mo). Pricing verified at
  elevenlabs.io/pricing: Free 10k credits (NO commercial use) · Starter $6/30k ·
  Creator $22/121k · Pro $99/600k.
- Eleven v3 is ALSO on fal (`fal-ai/elevenlabs/tts/eleven-v3`) — but the fal
  endpoint is preset-voices-only (no Voice Design, no My Voices), so the native
  account is the primary; fal is metered spillover once voices exist.
- ⚠ Not verified end-to-end on kiai/grunts: tags are voice-dependent per their own
  docs. Gate a ~10-bark test batch on ONE fighter before running all nine.

## FAL-NATIVE PATH: ChatterboxHD (Resemble) — already on your FAL_KEY
- `resemble-ai/chatterboxhd/text-to-speech` ($0.025/1k chars) +
  `resemble-ai/chatterboxhd/speech-to-speech`, curl flow you already run.
- Clone each fighter from ~5 s of reference audio (seed the references from
  ElevenLabs Voice Design output), keep the 9 reference wavs in the repo.
  `exaggeration` (0.25-2.0) is the effort/delivery knob.
- **The scream cheat code:** the speech-to-speech endpoint converts a grunt YOU
  record into the fighter's cloned voice — how real studios do efforts, and the
  most reliable KO-scream route on this list. Ideal for Mokurai's breath kit,
  where text-to-speech is weakest.

## BUDGET PATH: Fish Audio OpenAudio S1 — $15 per 1M chars (~11× cheaper)
- Strongest text-driven bark vocabulary of any current TTS: `(screaming)`,
  `(shouting)`, `(panting)`, `(groaning)`, `(painful)`, `(furious)` are
  first-class markers. Cloned voice IDs persist per fighter.
- `s1-mini` is open-weight (HuggingFace: fishaudio/openaudio-s1-mini) — local, $0,
  fixed seeds = fully reproducible voices. Check cloned-voice commercial terms at
  signup.

## THE BARK WORKFLOW (primary tool)
1. Voice Design once per fighter with the casting prompts above → save to My Voices.
2. Barks = tag + onomatopoeia, never a bare word: `[shouts] HYAAA!` ·
   `[grunts] Hrrgh!` · `[gasps] Ugh—` · `[screams] AAAGH…`. Punctuation drives
   intensity: caps, em-dash cutoffs, ellipses for dying falls.
3. Stability LOW for barks (wilder takes); raise it for intro/victory lines on the
   same voice.
4. Batch 5-10 takes per bark per fighter and cherry-pick — it's a casting session,
   not a render.
5. Wordless clips that flatten into speech → Sound Effects model, or record the
   grunt yourself and push it through ChatterboxHD speech-to-speech on fal.
6. Export wav, name by fighter/move (`ember_kiai_01.wav`), and run the one-fighter
   test gate before the full nine.

**Per-character tool notes:** Mokurai is a wordless kit — do him via the
speech-to-speech route (your own breath/growls converted) or Sound Effects, not
plain TTS. The Executioner needs no mask FILTER — he ships as TWO designed voices
(masked + human, owner Sep 15 2026), both exported clean: no low-pass, no reverb.
