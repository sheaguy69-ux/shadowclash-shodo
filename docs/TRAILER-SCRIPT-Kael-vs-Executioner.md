# TRAILER SCRIPT — "HE NEVER LOOKED UP"
## Kael vs the Executioner · 30 seconds

**Handoff doc.** Everything below is sourced from the ShadowClash Story Bible and the
shipped move list. Nothing is invented. Names, pronouns, colours and forbidden elements
are canon and must not be changed.

---

## THE PREMISE (one line)

**Kael challenges him. The Executioner never accepts.**

The fight is one-sided not because Kael is weak — he is the most *complete* fighter in the
pit — but because the Executioner is somewhere else the entire time, fighting something
inside the mask, and beats him anyway on reflex alone.

**The whole trailer is built on one mechanical fact: the Executioner never initiates.**
Every answer he gives is a **counter** — a parry, a slip, a reprisal. He does not throw a
single move of his own until the last second. That is the gap between their experience,
shown instead of stated.

**⛔ FORBIDDEN — will get the shot rejected**

  No oversized-yellow body drift; body and limbs stay near-black.
- **Executioner:** no dual weapons. Horns must read as part of the hood, never as separate
  floating objects. Orange only on scarf, sash, eyes.
- Both: glowing **featureless angled eyes**. No faces, no mouths, no pupils.

## WHY THESE TWO (the material)

- Kael is **the last student of a school that no longer exists.** His master taught him both
  blades because *a fighter who only knows one answer is only half awake.* It made him the
  most complete and **the least feared** man in the pit.
- He came down here **chasing someone, not the light.** He still believes the lantern can be
  carried back up and shared. Every fighter down here is proof nobody agrees.
- The Executioner **was the law's last word** — sanctioned, masked, paid. He ended people
  officially. He was good enough that **the mask stopped coming off between jobs.**
- Somewhere in the years **the warrants stopped mattering and the swing kept going.** He
  tells himself the light is a sentence he intends to carry out.
- **"Everyone else has met him before, and none of those meetings were fair."**

They have no blood feud. That is the point. Kael is a name on no list.

## THE SCRIPT — 30 SECONDS

> Sound design in *italics*. On-screen text in **BOLD CAPS**. Timecodes are hard.

---

**0:00 – 0:04 — THE APPROACH**

Wide. The pit. Kael walks in from frame left, gold catching the low light, both swords
sheathed. He is small in the frame and the frame knows it.

*Footsteps. Nothing else.*

**TEXT (small, bottom-left):** THE LAST STUDENT OF A SCHOOL THAT NO LONGER EXISTS

---

**0:04 – 0:08 — THE CHALLENGE**

Cut in. Kael draws — **long sword and short sword**, the cross stance. Squares up. This is
the most confident he will look all trailer.

*Two blades leaving two sheaths. Clean.*

**TEXT:** HE CAME DOWN CHASING SOMEONE

---

**0:08 – 0:12 — NO ANSWER**

Cut to the Executioner. Idle. Greatsword point-down. **He is not looking at Kael.** Hold
this longer than is comfortable — three full seconds of a man not reacting.

Push in slowly on the horned mask. The orange eyes flicker — not at Kael, at something
behind them. His DREAD glow ticks up one stage on its own, unprompted.

*A low sub-bass hum. A heartbeat that is not Kael's.*

**TEXT:** THE MASK STOPPED COMING OFF BETWEEN JOBS

---

**0:12 – 0:20 — EVERYTHING KAEL HAS**

The longest block in the trailer, and it is all Kael. Fast cuts, no slow-motion. He empties
his kit:

1. **Twin Cyclone** (Fwd+Heavy) — both blades, closing distance.
   → The Executioner **blocks**. Doesn't move his feet.
2. **Low-high Scissor** (Down+Heavy) — the mixup that beats most of the roster.
   → **SHADOW SLIP REVERSAL.** The Executioner i-frames backward. *He only counters if he
   read the swing.* He read it.
3. **Travelling Cross Slash** (Fwd+Special) — Kael crosses the gap himself.
   → **IRON GUARD REPRISAL.** Heavy straight out of the block. A riposte, not an attack.
4. **Niten Parry** (Back+S) — Kael's signature. The wakizashi catches, the katana answers.
   → It catches **nothing.** The Executioner didn't swing. Kael parried empty air.

*Steel, steel, steel — and under it, the same unbothered heartbeat, never speeding up.*

**TEXT (cutting between hits, one word per hit):** HE / IS / NOT / EVEN / HERE

---

**0:20 – 0:26 — THE ONE THAT SHOULD HAVE WORKED**

Kael goes to his last resort: the **Rising Twin Fang** (Down+Special) — **the launcher with
the invulnerable startup.** The move that cannot be beaten cleanly. Airborne, both blades
up, gold against the dark. Give it the trailer's only slow-motion, one beat of it.

The Executioner is **already gone.** He slipped before the invulnerability started, because
he moved on the *input*, not the animation.

Kael lands. Off balance. His back is open.

*The heartbeat stops. Total silence.*

---

**0:26 – 0:30 — THE EXECUTION**

The Executioner turns his head — **the first time all trailer he has looked at Kael.**

DREAD 3. One flash from the sheath. **The Execution.**

Cut to black on the frame *before* contact. Never show the hit.

*One sound. Then nothing.*

**TITLE CARD:** SHADOWCLASH

**Beat. Then, small, under the logo:**
NONE OF THOSE MEETINGS WERE FAIR

---

## THE FINAL FRAME (optional 2s tag)

Executioner walking out of frame, greatsword already sheathed, having never once broken
stride. The gold on the floor behind him is out of focus.

*No music sting. Let it end flat. He would.*

## HARD RULES FOR WHOEVER CUTS THIS

1. **The Executioner throws no offensive move until 0:26.** Everything before it is a block,
   a slip, or a riposte. If he attacks first anywhere in the cut, the trailer's whole idea
   is dead.
2. **No slow-motion except the Rising Twin Fang at 0:20.** One use, or it means nothing.
3. **Never show the finishing blow.** Cut on the frame before contact.
4. **The mask never comes off.** Nobody alive has seen under it, and that stays true here.
5. **Cel animation cuts — never cross-fades.** House style, non-negotiable.
6. **Kael is never made to look incompetent.** He executes every move perfectly. That is
   what makes it hurt. The gap is experience, not skill.
7. **He/him for both.** Stated because it has been gotten wrong before and reached a
   shipped screen.
