## The board names three of his stances

**Tachi Seiho** — "correct method of the long sword". Three rows, six beats each:

1. **Sassen** *(fingertip / pushing the tip)* — blade extended straight at the opponent
2. **Hasso Hidari** — sword carried high on the left
3. **Hasso Migi** — sword carried high on the right

Same relationship as the Executioner's katana board: named technique rows drawn as a
reference document, at a resolution too small to pack. The techniques wanted in the game come
back as their own full-width strips; the board is what those strips get generated against.
