# EMBER — SHUKO / TEKKO-KAGI TECHNIQUES

Two reference boards naming **six claw techniques**, six beats each — 36 frames total.
Archived, not packed. SHEET_V stays 575.

These are ash-grey Ember with the claws — the named vocabulary for the kit the Ghost Killer
disarm ships on (`89d2bca`).

## ⛔ But the faces on these boards are NOT the killer form

**Owner ruling, Aug 21 2026:** *"the form is gone completely, his killer form is when he have
his whole face wrapped up."* Two things follow, and the second is a question for the owner.

**The green form is deleted** — see `../../new-style-actionsets-aug20/DELETED-green-form/`.

**The killer form's tell is a fully wrapped face, no visible eye.** All nine strips in
`../strips/` show exactly that: a pale wrap band across the face and a dark void where the
eye would be. **These two boards do not** — both draw a glowing pale-green eye inside the
hood, the same as the deleted green form did.

So either the boards are drawing the wrong face and want a re-render with the wrap, **or**
ash-grey-with-an-eye is a second, non-killer state that now needs its own name. That is the
owner's call, not one to infer from the art.

*(Recorded honestly: two attempts to classify this automatically both failed — the first
excluded a bright low-saturation eye from the body mask by construction so it could never
find one, the second counted claw glints and FX sparks as eyes. The call above is made by
looking at the crops, not from a metric.)*

| # | technique | japanese | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Downward Tiger Rake | *Tora Tsume Tensho* | explosive downward diagonal swipe using body weight; starts above the ear, tears across the face or neck in a hooking arc |
| 2 | Leaping Pounce Strike | *Tobi Tora Geki* | forward lunging **double**-hand thrust, both clawed hands into chest or throat from a low spring-loaded stance |
| 3 | Horizontal Crescent Tear | *Mawa-shi Rip* | wide horizontal hooking slash driven by hip rotation; throat, neck or ribcage |

physical body language rather than game jargon.
