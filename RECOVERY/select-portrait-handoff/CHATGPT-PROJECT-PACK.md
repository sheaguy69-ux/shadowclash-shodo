# SHADOW CLASH — Context Pack for ChatGPT (art & design work)
Upload this file + the images in refs/ to a ChatGPT Project. This is the
single source of truth; when chat memory conflicts with this pack, the pack wins.

## What the game is
SHADOW CLASH — a 2D chibi ninja brawler (web, canvas). Fast, lethal-feeling
hits; deception mechanics (decoy logs, counters, teleports) are the identity.
Art style law: **clean high-contrast 2D CEL ILLUSTRATION — heavy black
outlines, flat cel shading, controlled dark palette, glowing featureless
angled eyes. NEVER pixel art. NO NEON, no cyan/magenta glow — light sources
are moonlight, fire, embers, paper lanterns.** Outfits are battle-worn:
ripped scarves, frayed hoods, torn hems.

## The story in one breath (private — do not reuse outside this project)
The light of the world was stolen and hung like a lantern over a pit. The pit
summons six fighters who already share blood grudges; only one can carry the
light out. Three guardians stand between them and it — the climb order is
EXILE (the toll), then MOKURAI (the patience), then ONI — the final boss,
the pit's own hunger, standing directly in front of the light.

## The NINE fighters — look + weapon canon (match the ref IMAGES exactly)
THE SIX (trailer refs are canon):
1. EXECUTIONER — hulking; maroon/dark-crimson hood, TALL curved horns, glowing
   red eyes, torn cape. Weapon: massive broad single-edged blade in a
   traditional Japanese form, planted point-down like a headsman at rest.
2. MIZU — calm keeper; purple hood/robes, white angled eyes. Weapon: LONG
   wooden bō staff. Mist curls at her feet. She holds lines, never crosses them.
3. SHIN — lean infiltrator; dark teal-green hood, cyan eyes, bandaged arms.
   Weapon: classic FOUR-POINT hira-shuriken (+ a thin wire). **NEVER any
   blade — a blade can be traced back to a hand.**
4. TSUBASA — precision incarnate; spiky black hair with RED streaks, ripped
   red scarf, black outfit w/ red trim. Weapon: TWO knives (tantō), crossed.
5. EMBER — feral survivor of a fire; dark-green hood, torn scarf tails, green
   blazing eyes. Weapon: TWO three-bladed steel claw gauntlets (tekko-kagi).
   Fights low, pounces, climbs walls.
6. KAEL — the complete student; black hood with GOLD trim, gold tattered
   scarf, gold-orange eyes. Weapon: one LONG katana + one SHORT blade —
   the length difference must be obvious at a glance.
THE THREE (approved masters are canon):
7. MOKURAI, THE SAGE OF NOTHINGNESS — massive serene stone-faced monk, ruby
   forehead jewel, ochre robes, heavy prayer beads. NO weapon — open-palm
   warding mudra, bare hands and bead-wrapped fists. He has NO bo staff and
   never did. The word "Buddha" never ships.
8. ONI, THE FOUNDER — owner's FINAL bible, Aug 9 2026
   (`RECOVERY/oni-founder/ONI-BIBLE-FINAL.png`): ragged black hood, WHITE demon
   mask with three claw gouges and two glowing RED slit eyes, two horns,
   ash-cloth wrappings on BOTH hands and forearms, dark gunmetal armour, TWO
   KATANAS sheathed crossed on his back, and a four-bladed tekko-kagi claw on
   his RIGHT HAND ONLY (left hand wrapped, empty). He carries NO club — the
   kanabō is retired. Red #D71302 on the eyes is the only hot colour; the rest
   is black, ash and worn metal. PURPLE IS BANNED on Oni.
9. EXILE — the one-eyed toll collector; huge wild BLACK hair with GREY
   streaks, a weathered WOODEN PLANK tied over one eye painted with red
   kanji (流罪), black face mask, purple scarf, dark torn rags, bandaged
   arms. Weapon: kama sickle + long chain with a round iron fundo weight.

## Working rules with the owner (Anthony)
- He approves ALL art by looking at a montage strip BEFORE it's used. Always
  produce a review strip; never call something done without his eyes on it.
- Match the reference IMAGE, not a text memory of the character. When output
  and reference disagree, the output is wrong.
- Max 2 generation attempts per piece, then stop and describe the failure.
- Deliverables for select portraits: full-body signature stance, transparent
  or clean solid-white background, ~800x1050+, one consistent style across
  all nine.
